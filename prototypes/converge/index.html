Design convergence · November 2026

Eight cuts.
One local workbench.

The 30-page cut gave the direction. This set removes the home screens, inboxes, checklists, saved spaces and sharing artifacts, and turns the surviving ideas into one graph-first web shell with four modes: focus, path, impact, compare. The table, overview and command palette become supporting surfaces rather than destinations.
