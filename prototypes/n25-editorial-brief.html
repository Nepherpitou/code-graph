The checkout request enters at CheckoutController.checkout and descends through validation, pricing, order creation, authorization, and capture. That is six functions, but only one of them is both a hotspot and the pivot of the failure path.

createOrder reserves inventory before authorizing payment. It has three direct callers and reaches four downstream functions; two of its five call sites are untested. The graph shows the route; the risk score explains why it matters.
